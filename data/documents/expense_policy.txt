Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: FIN-001
Classification: Internal
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This policy explains how employees can request
reimbursement for approved business expenses.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES

Employees may request reimbursement for:
- Business travel
- Approved accommodation
- Client meetings
- Training and certification costs
- Office supplies

3. APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS

Expenses above $500 require manager approval.

4. REIMBURSEMENT PROCESS

Employees must submit receipts within 30 days.

5. DATA PRIVACY

Employee financial information is confidential.
Individual salary records must not be disclosed
through the expense reimbursement process.

6. CONTACT

Employees should contact the Finance department
for reimbursement questions.
